Social Publisher Bot

A desktop-based workflow for preparing and scheduling photo carousel posts to social media accounts authorized by their owners.

How it works

  1. The account owner chooses images and writes a caption.
  2. The desktop application prepares the content and queues posts according to a schedule.
  3. Publishing to a platform is available only after the account owner authorizes access and the platform grants the required permissions.

Current availability

The Instagram workflow is being tested. TikTok integration is in development and is not yet available for public use or approved for direct posting. The application is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok or Instagram.

For support or privacy inquiries, contact the application operator using the contact email provided in the app's developer registration. A public support email will be added before submission for review.
