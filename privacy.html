Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This policy describes the intended operation of Social Publisher Bot, a desktop-based social media scheduling tool. TikTok integration is under development and is not yet operational.

Information processed

When connected and authorized, the application may process platform account identifiers, account names, access tokens, images selected by the account owner, captions, scheduling preferences, publishing status, and error logs. It does not request account passwords.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate authorized accounts, prepare uploads, schedule posts, prevent duplicate publishing, and troubleshoot errors. Content is not sold to advertisers.

Storage and service providers

The current desktop workflow stores configuration and publishing state on the operator's local computer. Image uploads for Instagram use Cloudinary to host media accessible to the platform. Instagram and any future TikTok integration process content according to their respective policies. The implementation and providers may change; this policy must be updated before new functionality is launched.

Retention and deletion

The operator can remove locally stored credentials, media, and publishing records and revoke platform permissions. Copies uploaded to third-party services may need to be deleted separately through those services. Publishing history may remain on social platforms until deleted there.

Security

Access credentials should be stored outside public repositories and never included in the website source. Access is restricted to authorized accounts.

Contact and requests

For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact the application operator through the support email provided in the developer registration. A public contact address must be added here before submitting the application for review.

Changes

This policy may be revised as the application develops. The effective date will be updated when material changes occur.
